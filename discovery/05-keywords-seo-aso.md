> **Date:** 2026-03-30
> **For:** Falcly discovery

## Executive Summary

Falcly's keyword strategy spans two markets (US and Philippines) and three channels (ASO, website SEO, paid search). The credit card management keyword space has moderate competition — dominated by CardPointers, MaxRewards, and YNAB. Falcly's unique advantage: billing cycle and timing-related keywords have almost zero competition because no app targets them.
