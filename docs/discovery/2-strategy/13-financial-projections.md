# Falcly — Financial Projections

> **Date:** 2026-03-30
> **For:** Falcly discovery

---

## Executive Summary

Falcly's financial model is built on a two-phase market entry: Philippines (Year 1, organic) followed by US expansion (Year 2). PH validates the concept and proves willingness-to-pay. US is the inflection point — users pay 3x more, market is 35x larger. Combined, Year 2 end state reaches ~$140K ARR — solo-project independence territory.

## 6. The 100-User Milestone

**When:** Realistically Week 2–3 after launch.

**How:** One well-timed post to r/PHCreditCards can pull 100+ signups in a single weekend. The subreddit has 261K members and sees weekly threads about billing cycle confusion, cutoff dates, and statement timing — the exact pain Falcly solves.

The pain point is already being voiced. Falcly just needs to show up with a working answer.

**Why it matters:** 100 users validates the concept, seeds early reviews, and generates first word-of-mouth. This is the first milestone to hit before optimizing anything else.

---

## 7. Key Insight

PH Year 1 is the proof-of-concept phase. ~$1,650/mo MRR at M12 isn't independence money — but it proves users will pay for billing cycle intelligence, establishes App Store rankings and reviews, and gives a working product to launch into the US.

US launch in Year 2 is the inflection point:
- US users pay **$4.99/mo** vs. ₱149/mo (~$2.61) — **1.9x higher ARPU**
- US credit card holders: **175M+** vs. PH's ~13.9M — **12x larger addressable base**
- Post-Mint displacement still active — 3.6M users lost their app in 2024 with no direct replacement

**$11,685 MRR / ~$140K ARR at end of Year 2 = solo-project independence territory.** One developer, no employees, no office.

## 8. Revenue Model Summary

| Plan | US | PH | Notes |
|------|----|----|-------|
| Free | $0 | ₱0 | 2 cards, full safe-to-swipe logic |
| Pro Monthly | $4.99/mo | ₱149/mo | Unlimited cards |

**App Store fees:** 15–30% cut on in-app purchases (15% for developers under $1M/yr via Apple Small Business Program and Google equivalent). Projections above are gross; net would be ~85% of listed figures.
