# Falcly — Competitor Deep Dive

> **Date:** 2026-03-30
> **For:** Falcly discovery

---

## Executive Summary

Falcly's competitive landscape splits into three tiers: direct competitors (billing cycle / card management tools — very few), indirect competitors (budgeting and personal finance apps), and platform competitors (bank apps and super-apps). The core finding remains: **no existing app answers "Is it safe to use my card right now?" with cycle-aware, cash-aware, deterministic logic.** This is Falcly's white space.

Key 2025–2026 developments: CardPointers raised prices to $7.50/mo ($90/yr), MaxRewards settled at ~$9/mo ($54–60/yr), YNAB and Monarch both charge $14.99/mo, and Copilot remains iOS-only US-only. None added billing cycle intelligence.

## Tier 1: Direct — Credit Card Management Tools

### CardPointers
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Platform** | iOS, Android, Chrome/Safari extensions |
| **Pricing** | Free + CardPointers+ at $7.50/mo or $90/yr (50% off sale through March 2026: $45/yr) |
| **Focus** | Rewards optimization — which card gives the most points |
| **Users** | ~1M+ downloads |

**Key Features:** Tracks 5,000+ credit card category bonuses, auto-enrolls card-linked offers (Amex, Citi, Chase), browser extension shows best card while shopping, annual fee calendar, due date reminders, credit utilization display.

**Strengths:** Best-in-class rewards optimization, broad card support, browser integration, Apple Watch support.

**Weaknesses:** Primary focus is rewards (which card), NOT timing (when to use). No "safe to swipe" logic. No billing cycle timeline. No cash-flow awareness. No statement balance projection. US-centric.

**Threat to Falcly:** Low. Different core use case (rewards vs. timing). Could be complementary.

---

### MaxRewards
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Platform** | iOS, Android, Chrome extension |
| **Pricing** | $9/mo minimum ($54–60/yr) |
| **Focus** | Rewards tracking + "best card" recommendations |

**Key Features:** "Best card for use" tool, auto-activation of quarterly bonuses and offers, credit utilization ratio display, rewards balance tracking, business spending categorization (Platinum tier).

**Strengths:** Supports every major issuer (Amex, Chase, Citi, Discover, Capital One, BofA, Wells Fargo), business card support.

**Weaknesses:** Cards regularly unsync (2–4 times/month for Chase and Amex). No billing cycle intelligence. No safe-to-swipe indicator. No cash awareness. No statement date projection. Requires ongoing maintenance.

**Threat to Falcly:** Low. Rewards-focused, not timing-focused.

---

### Kudos
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Platform** | Browser extension primarily |
| **Pricing** | Free |
| **Focus** | Browser-based "best card" at checkout |

**Strengths:** Free, lightweight, doesn't require card linking. **Weaknesses:** Browser-only, no mobile app, no billing cycle features, no cash awareness. **Threat:** Very low.

### Credit Card Bill Reminder Apps (Google Play)
- **Cardly Reminder**, **Credit Card Bill Reminder**, and similar apps
- Free, Android-only, pure reminder utilities
- No cycle intelligence, no utilization tracking, no safe-to-swipe logic
- Dated UIs, minimal features
- **Threat:** Very low. Falcly replaces these entirely.

## Tier 2: Indirect — Budgeting & Personal Finance Apps

### YNAB (You Need A Budget)
| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Pricing** | $14.99/mo or $109/yr |
| **Users** | ~1M paid |
| **Focus** | Zero-based budgeting |

**Key Features:** Every dollar gets a "job," goal tracking, bank syncing, debt paydown tools, loan calculator.

**What it does NOT do:** Track billing cycles, statement dates, utilization timing, or answer "is it safe to swipe." YNAB explicitly doesn't track billing cycles — confirmed by users and support docs.

**Threat to Falcly:** Low-medium as budget alternative. No overlap on core feature. Users may use both.

## Tier 3: Platform Competitors (Philippines)

### GCash
- 94M+ registered users, dominant PH mobile wallet
- Basic card payment features (pay bills via GCash)
- No credit card cycle tracking, no utilization awareness, no safe-to-swipe
- Pursuing IPO (2026, delayed to H2)

### Maya
- GCash's primary competitor, digital bank license
- Pursuing IPO (2026, dual US/PSE listing planned)
- Offers savings, crypto, insurance — no credit card cycle intelligence

### Bank Apps (BDO, BPI, RCBC, UnionBank)
- Each bank's app shows that bank's card only
- No cross-bank view, no cycle intelligence, no safe-to-swipe logic
- The structural problem: users have 2–4 cards from different banks with different cutoff dates

**Falcly's opportunity:** None of these platform players solve the multi-card billing cycle coordination problem. They each show one card. Falcly shows all cards with timing intelligence across banks.
