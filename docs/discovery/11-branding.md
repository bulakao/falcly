# Falcly — Branding

> **Date:** 2026-03-30
> **For:** Falcly discovery

---

## Executive Summary

Falcly's brand is built on precision, speed, and sharp intelligence — mirroring the app's core value proposition: instant, definitive answers about your credit card. The name, color system, typography, and voice all reinforce one thing: clarity without noise.

---

## 1. Name & Domain

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| **App name** | Falcly |
| **Domain** | falcly.com |
| **Domain status** | ✅ Acquired |
| **Domain cost** | ~$11/yr (Porkbun) |

### Name Rationale

Falcon = speed, precision, sharp vision — maps directly to the app's core behavior: instant yes/no on whether it's safe to swipe. "Falcly" is 2 syllables, punchy, techy without being generic.

30+ names were explored and shortlisted during naming research — including: owlert, noctra, spyglo, cyclevu, haukly, cardvu, swipely, billio, cyclr, statemint, cardclr, and others. Falcly won on three axes:

1. **Brandability** — unique, memorable, no direct confusion with existing fintech brands
2. **Availability** — domain acquired at ~$11/yr, no trademark conflicts found
3. **Resonance** — falcon imagery naturally communicates the app's speed and precision

## 2. Brand Identity

### Tagline

> **"Know before you swipe."**

**Alternative taglines:**
- "Precise. Every cycle."
- "Sharp eyes on your cycle."
- "Your card, in sharp focus."

### Brand Story

Falcly watches your billing cycle so you don't have to. One tap, one answer — SAFE, CAUTION, or RISKY.

### Tone

Direct, confident, brief. Like a trusted advisor who gives you a straight answer — no corporate filler, no hedging, no fluff.

## 3. Color System — Black & Premium

**Philosophy:** Premium black-forward design. Think luxury fintech (Robinhood, not Chase). The SAFE / CAUTION / RISKY status colors aren't just UI elements — they're the brand. Users will associate those three colors with Falcly instantly.

### Light Mode

| Role | Color Name | Hex |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Background | Off-white | `#F8F9FA` |
| Surface | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Primary text | Near black | `#0D0D0D` |
| Secondary text | Dark gray | `#4A4A4A` |
| Border | Light gray | `#E5E5E5` |
| Primary action | Deep black | `#0D0D0D` |
| Accent | Falcon amber | `#F5A623` |
| **SAFE** | Emerald | `#2ECC71` |
| **CAUTION** | Amber | `#F39C12` |
| **RISKY** | Crimson | `#E74C3C` |

### Dark Mode

| Role | Color Name | Hex |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Background | True black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Surface | Dark slate | `#141414` |
| Card surface | Elevated | `#1C1C1C` |
| Primary text | Off-white | `#F0F0F0` |
| Secondary text | Muted | `#888888` |
| Border | Subtle | `#2A2A2A` |
| Primary action | White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Accent | Falcon amber | `#F5A623` |
| **SAFE** | Emerald | `#2ECC71` |
| **CAUTION** | Amber | `#F39C12` |
| **RISKY** | Crimson | `#E74C3C` |

### Color Usage Notes

- The falcon amber (`#F5A623`) is used sparingly — accent elements, icons, CTA highlights. It should feel premium, not cluttered.
- SAFE/CAUTION/RISKY carry full saturation in both modes — they must read instantly, even in peripheral vision.
- Dark mode is the preferred design surface for initial marketing assets (screenshots, App Store previews). Black-forward fintech reads premium.

## 4. Logo Direction

### Concept
Minimal falcon silhouette — head or eye only, not a full bird. The logo should feel like precision engineering, not a mascot.

### Variants
| Variant | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **App icon** | Amber falcon eye (`#F5A623`) on deep black background (`#0A0A0A`) — single focal point, readable at 60×60px |
| **Wordmark** | "Falcly" in DM Sans Bold with falcon amber accent on the "F" ascender |
| **Lettermark** | Stylized **F** with falcon wing sweep integrated into the letterform |
| **Monochrome** | White version for dark backgrounds; black version for light |

### Icon Design Principle
One focal point. The amber eye on black. Recognizable at notification icon size (20×20px). No gradients. No complexity.

## 5. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Why |
|-----|----------|-----|
| Headlines | **Nunito** | Rounded, friendly, warm — pairs well with the mascot while staying clean |
| Body | **Inter** | Highly legible at small sizes — essential for mobile |
| Card numbers / data | **JetBrains Mono** (or system monospace) | Tabular alignment, fintech credibility |

Both Nunito and Inter are free on Google Fonts.

### Type Scale (Mobile)
- Screen title: Nunito Bold, 24px
- Section header: Nunito SemiBold, 18px
- Body: Inter Regular, 14px
- Caption/label: Inter Regular, 12px
- Status badge (SAFE/CAUTION/RISKY): Nunito Bold, 13px — caps, tight tracking

## 6. Voice & Tone

### Do

| ✅ Do | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Be direct | "You're safe to swipe." |
| Be confident | "RISKY. Statement closes in 2 days." |
| Be brief | Every screen fits in a glance |
| Use numbers | "₱12,400 available. 6 days to statement." |
| Be definitive | "Best card: BPI Petron. 18 days left." |

### Don't

| ❌ Don't | Example to avoid |
|----------|-----------------|
| Corporate jargon | "Based on your current utilization metrics..." |
| Fluffy filler | "Great choice! You're on top of your finances!" |
| Hedging | "It appears that approximately..." |
| Passive voice | "Your card may potentially be at risk" |
| Emoji overload | "🎉 You're safe to use your card today! 💳✨" |

### Copy Examples by Screen

| Screen | Copy |
|--------|------|
| Safe-to-swipe result | "SAFE. 14 days to statement. 28% utilization." |
| Caution warning | "CAUTION. Statement closes in 7 days. Watch utilization." |
| Risky alert | "RISKY. 2 days to statement. Do not swipe." |
| Best card pick | "Use BPI Petron today. Lowest cycle pressure." |
| Onboarding | "Add your card. Get your answer." |
| Paywall | "Unlimited cards. ₱149/mo." |
