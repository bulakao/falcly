**Core Intelligence Features**

| Feature | Falcly | CardPointers | MaxRewards | YNAB | Monarch | Copilot | Simplifi | GCash |
|---------|--------|-------------|------------|------|---------|---------|----------|-------|
| **Swipe Impact Analysis** | ✅ Enter proposed spend → see projected statement, net available cash, payoff plan, interest cost | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Flexible Horizon Projection** | ✅ Projects forward based on known fixed events (installments, subscriptions, annual fees) — not a fixed window | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Net Available Cash Calculation** | ✅ Liquid cash + income before due - committed expenses - auto-payments - subscriptions | ❌ | ❌ | ⚠️ Tracks budgets but not card-specific available cash for payoff | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Installment tracking** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Subscription / recurring charge tracking** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| **Annual fee reminders** | ✅ | ✅ | ⚡ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Payment strategy recommendations (avalanche/snowball/minimum/full)** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Auto-payment awareness** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| **Cash-dependent payment planning** | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

**Notes:**
- Monarch and Copilot/Simplifi track subscriptions but do not tie them to billing cycle impact or safe-to-swipe logic
- CardPointers has an annual fee calendar but only for US cards; no PH bank support
- No competitor combines installment tracking with cycle intelligence or cash-aware payoff planning
- Falcly's payment intelligence is uniquely integrated with cycle timing — e.g., subscriptions due before statement date adjust the safe-to-swipe score
