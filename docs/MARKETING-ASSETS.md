### App Store / Play Store Description

**iOS Subtitle (30 chars max):**
> Know before you swipe.

**Play Store Short Description (80 chars max):**
> Credit card timing intelligence. SAFE, CAUTION, or RISKY — instantly.

**Full Description (draft):**
```
Is it safe to swipe your card today?

Falcly answers in one tap. SAFE. CAUTION. RISKY — for every card, every cycle, instantly.

WHAT FALCLY DOES
• Shows SAFE / CAUTION / RISKY status for all your cards
• Swipe Impact: enter any amount, see exactly how it affects your next bill
• Billing cycle timeline — know how far you are from statement close
• Best card recommendation — always know which card to use today
• Cycle-aware transaction log — more accurate than your bank app in real time
• Net available cash — what you can actually afford to pay, not just your limit

WHO IT'S FOR
Filipino credit card holders who know their stuff but can't hold 3 overlapping billing cycles in their head. (Also great for US cardholders with multiple cards.)

WHAT IT'S NOT
Not a budgeting app. Not an expense tracker. Not a bank app. Just one answer: safe to swipe, or not?

Free to use. Falcly Pro unlocks unlimited cards.
```
