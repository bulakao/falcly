## Core Value Proposition

**Falcly tells you which card to use before you swipe — so you never get a surprise bill.**

---

## The Problem

Most credit card users are reasonably responsible. They're not irresponsible spenders — they're **mis-timers**.

They swipe on the 28th thinking "I'll pay it next month" — not knowing their statement closes on the 30th and it hits *this* bill. They check their balance and think they're fine, not realizing their payday comes *after* the due date. They have 3 cards and pick the wrong one out of habit.

The result: a statement that's higher than expected. Every. Single. Month.

A note or spreadsheet gives you *data*. It tells you when your cycle closes. But it doesn't tell you what that means *right now*, for *this* purchase, across *all* your cards.

---

## The Solution

Falcly collapses the math into a single signal at the moment of decision.

You're at the counter. You have 3 cards. Falcly tells you:
- Which card is SAFE to use today
- Which cycle this charge lands on
- Whether you can actually cover it given your payday and expenses

One answer. No math. No surprises.

---

## What Makes It Different

- **Not a budgeting app** — you don't need to log every transaction
- **Not an expense tracker** — you don't categorize your coffee
- **Pure billing cycle intelligence** — the one question that matters: *is it safe to swipe right now?*

---

## Who It's For

People who are responsible with money but still occasionally get caught off guard by their statement. They have 2–4 credit cards, they pay on time (or try to), and they've wondered at least once: *"Wait, does this charge hit this month or next?"*

PH-first: semi-monthly paydays, multiple cards, no bank sync available — Falcly works entirely offline, no account linking required.
