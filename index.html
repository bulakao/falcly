How does "Best choice" work?

Each card gets a score (lower = better to use). The lowest-scoring SAFE card is the "Best choice."

Base scoreUtilization % (0–100)
+50≤ 7 days to statement
+200≤ 2 days to statement
+100CAUTION status
+300RISKY status

In short: use the card with the lowest utilization and the most time before its statement closes.

If no card is SAFE, none gets the "Best choice" badge.
